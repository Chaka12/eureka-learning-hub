About Us

Where every learner finds their spark

Eureka Learning Hub is a community-rooted learning centre built on a simple but powerful belief: words shape worlds. When a child says, “I can,” they begin to. When a family says, “We believe in you,” they grow. Our name and our motto — Say It and It Becomes — are the heart of how we teach.

We offer tutoring, enrichment, and exam preparation for primary and secondary learners, delivered by passionate educators who know each student by name. Whether your child needs to catch up, keep up, or leap ahead, we meet them where they are and walk with them forward.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What ages do you teach?

We work with learners from age 4 through to upper secondary. Our early-learners programme focuses on ages 4–7, core tutoring covers primary and secondary, and our exam-prep classes support students through their high-stakes examinations.

How are classes structured?

Most students attend 1–3 sessions per week. Sessions are usually 60–90 minutes, with a maximum of 6 students per small group. We also offer one-on-one intensive tutoring for students who need focused attention.

How do you track progress?

Every student gets a personal learning plan and a baseline assessment. Parents receive written progress reports every term and informal check-ins much more often.

What are your fees?

Fees vary by programme. We keep our pricing transparent and offer sibling discounts and need-based scholarships. Contact us for a current fee schedule.

Where are you located?

Mankoaneng, Hlotse, Leribe, Lesotho.

How do we enrol?

Send us a message through the contact form below, message us on Facebook, or call us. We'll set up a free consultation, learn about your child, and recommend the right starting point.
